Community Guidelines

Respect, consent and safety on NELOA

HomePrivacyTerms

NELOA is an adults-only (18+) social discovery and live-audio community. These rules apply to profiles, posts, comments, chats, audio rooms, games and all other activity.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, hate or targeted humiliation.
  • Sexual exploitation, non-consensual sexual content, coercion or any content involving minors.
  • Impersonation, scams, spam, manipulated payments or attempts to move purchases outside approved channels.
  • Sharing another person's private information without permission.
  • Illegal activity, dangerous instructions, graphic violence or promotion of self-harm.
  • Evading blocks, bans, reporting systems or room-moderation controls.

Live-audio conduct

  • Only speak when you are on an authorized speaker seat/stage.
  • Follow the host/RJ's moderation controls. Hosts may mute, remove or report guests.
  • Do not record or redistribute another person's voice without lawful permission and consent.

Safety tools and enforcement

Use in-app block and report controls for users, content and room activity. NELOA may remove content, limit features, suspend or terminate accounts, preserve relevant evidence, and cooperate with lawful authorities where appropriate.

If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services. For NELOA support, email neloa.official.admin@gmail.com.
